Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026  ·  Last updated: July 28, 2026

Carbura is an independent mobile app that helps drivers compare fuel prices nearby. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing any government, ministry, regulator, or public authority. We collect the minimum data needed to deliver that service and do not sell your personal information to any third party.

1. Information We Collect

Data Why we collect it Stored?
Approximate location (coarse GPS or network) To show fuel stations near you and sort results by distance No — used in-session only, never sent to our servers
Device locale / language To pre-select your country on first launch and display the correct language No — read locally, never transmitted
Selected country & language preferences To remember your choice between app launches Yes — stored locally on your device only (AsyncStorage)
Recent city searches To show your search history for convenience Yes — stored locally on your device only

We do not require account creation, do not collect your name, email, or phone number, and do not run any servers that store your personal data.

2. How We Use It

The data listed above is used exclusively to:

  • Show you fuel stations sorted by proximity to your current location
  • Display prices in the correct currency and language for your selected country
  • Remember your country and language preference across sessions

We do not use your data for profiling, tracking, or any purpose beyond operating the app's core features.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

Carbura displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect and use the following to serve relevant ads:

  • Advertising ID (Android Ad ID / IDFA)
  • Approximate location (if you have granted location permission)
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • App usage signals (which screens you visit)

This data is processed by Google under their own privacy policy. You can review it at policies.google.com/privacy.

To opt out of interest-based advertising on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

4. Third-Party Data Sources

Carbura fetches fuel prices from official government open-data APIs and community-reported sources depending on the country selected. No personal data is sent to these services — requests contain only a country identifier and, where required for geographic filtering, a city name or bounding box derived from your selection.

Each country's source is also shown and linked inside the app on the About screen.

Country Source Notes
France data.economie.gouv.fr Ministère de l'Économie open data, "Prix des carburants en France" (Licence Ouverte / Etalab)
Germany creativecommons.tankerkoenig.de Tankerkönig API (CC BY 4.0), which republishes data from the Markttransparenzstelle für Kraftstoffe (MTS-K)
Austria spritpreisrechner.at E-Control Spritpreisrechner. Under Austrian price-transparency law, only the 5 cheapest stations may be shown per query

Spain geoportalgasolineras.es Ministerio para la Transición Ecológica, Geoportal de Gasolineras
United Kingdom fuelcosts.co.uk Republishes retailer prices open-sourced under the Motor Fuel Price (Open Data) Regulations 2025 (CMA Fuel Finder). Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0
Romania monitorulpreturilor.info Consiliul Concurenţei, via Monitorul Preţurilor
Sweden bensinpriser.nu bensinpriser.nu (Crownberry AB). Prices are reported by users and station owners

Poland paliwomapa.pl PaliwoMapa.pl. Prices are reported by drivers

Carbura does not collect, measure, or estimate prices itself. Every price shown is republished exactly as the source published it, with a timestamp. More countries may be added in future updates.

5. Data Retention

All data stored by Carbura lives locally on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Carbura → Storage → Clear Storage) or by uninstalling the app.

We have no server-side records of your preferences or searches to delete on your behalf.

6. Children's Privacy

Carbura is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

7. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, most data-subject rights (access, rectification, erasure) are exercisable directly on your device. If you have a question about data processed by Google AdMob, you can contact Google directly or use their Data & Privacy dashboard.

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have rights under the GDPR. If you are in California, you have rights under the CCPA. To exercise any right, or if you have any concern, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
